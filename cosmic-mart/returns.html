Return Policy
  • Gadgets & Electronics30 days
  • Fashion & Apparel14 days
  • Home & Lifestyle21 days
  • Cosmic Rewards Tier 1 & 2+7 extra days

Items must be in original or near-original condition. Opened digital & software products are non-returnable.

What Qualifies for a Return
  • Defective or malfunctioning product
  • Wrong item received
  • Item damaged during transit
  • Product not as described on the website
  • Unopened item (changed mind, within window)

Opened “changed mind” returns may qualify for store credit. Cosmic Rewards Tier 1 members receive a full refund regardless.

Refund & Shipping Timeline
  • Prepaid labelInstant
  • Refund to original payment3–5 business days
